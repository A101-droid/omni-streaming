# OmniStreaming

OmniStreaming is a decentralized music platform that revolutionizes artist monetization through dynamic rights tokenization and algorithmic royalty distribution. Unlike traditional streaming services that pay artists fractions of pennies per play, this platform creates individualized NFT contracts for each song that automatically adjust streaming prices based on real-time demand, artist reputation scores, and listener engagement metrics.

The platform implements a three-tier smart contract architecture consisting of Rights Registry Contracts for immutable ownership recording, Dynamic Pricing Oracles that calculate fair streaming costs using machine learning algorithms, and Instant Settlement Contracts that distribute payments within seconds of each play. Advanced technical features include cross-chain compatibility, audio fingerprinting stored on IPFS for IP protection, and automatic copyright enforcement with decentralized arbitration for dispute resolution.

Artists retain full ownership while earning 70-85% of streaming revenue compared to the industry standard of 15-25%, with built-in collaborative royalty splitting and fan investment opportunities through token staking. Listeners benefit from micropayment streaming where they only pay for content they actually consume, with AI-powered music discovery that rewards both listeners and artists for successful recommendations. The platform generates revenue through transaction fees, premium analytics dashboards, and white-label licensing for independent record labels seeking blockchain integration.
